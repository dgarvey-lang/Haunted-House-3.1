The Haunted House of Systems

Enter if you dare…

A ghost has scattered the 13 pieces of a picture all around this haunted house. Solve the problem behind each door to win its puzzle piece back.

  • Walk around the house and pick any door. Door numbers match the problem numbers on the paper worksheet. Show your work in the boxes, then press Check answer.
  • Get it right and the door gives you a puzzle piece 🧩.
  • Stuck? Press Help. The first press gives a hint, the next gives more help, and then the full solution. If you use More help or the full solution, the ghosts make you solve a similar problem on your own before the door gives up its piece.
  • Find all 13 pieces to finish the picture. Then copy your teacher code and turn it in with your name, spelled the same way you type it here.
  • 🔊 The house has spooky music and sound effects. Use the buttons at the top of the page to turn them off.
